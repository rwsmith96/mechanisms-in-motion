# Carla, start here

Everything you need to run Mechanisms in Motion with Rich is in one place. You do not need any technical setup, a GitHub account, or git. This page walks you through it in five minutes.

## 1. The two files that matter

- **The roadmap:** [mim-podcast-roadmap.pdf](mim-podcast-roadmap.pdf) — a six-page picture of the whole launch (the tools, the gear, the workflow, the timeline). Read this first.
- **The calendar:** [mim-recording-schedule.ics](mim-recording-schedule.ics) — the spine of the project. Import it once and your calendar tells you what to do, when, for every step.

## 2. How to get the files

Open the project page in any browser: **https://github.com/rwsmith96/mechanisms-in-motion**

You do not need an account. To download a file, click its name, then click the **Download** button (for the PDF) or the download icon (top-right of the file view). On a phone, tap the file, then the share/download icon.

If that is ever fiddly, just ask Rich to text or email you the two files. They are small.

## 3. Import the calendar (one time)

Download `mim-recording-schedule.ics`, then:

- **Google Calendar:** open Google Calendar on a computer, click the gear (Settings) > Import & Export > Import, choose the `.ics` file, pick your calendar, Import.
- **Apple Calendar (Mac/iPhone):** double-tap or open the `.ics` file; Calendar asks which calendar to add the events to; confirm.
- **Outlook:** File > Open & Export > Import/Export > Import an iCalendar (.ics) file.

All 16 events appear: the prep milestones, the eight recording sessions, and the weekly publish reminders. Times are Pacific.

## 4. How the calendar runs the project

Open any event (for example "MiM: Record Ep01" or "MiM DUE: First 4 guests"). In its notes you will find three things:

1. **DO** — the exact steps for that day.
2. **RIGHT LOOKS LIKE** — how to know it is done.
3. **ASSISTANT PROMPT** — a block of text you can copy and paste into Claude or ChatGPT, and it will walk you through the step.

So to do any step: open its calendar event, read the DO list, and if you want help, copy the ASSISTANT PROMPT into your AI assistant. That is the whole system.

## 5. Using the assistant prompt (any AI works)

The prompts are self-contained: you do not need to open any other file. Just:

1. Open the calendar event.
2. Copy the whole **ASSISTANT PROMPT** block from the notes.
3. Paste it into Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT.
4. It will know exactly what the step is and help you do it.

If you want the full background, the prompt also links to the project spec ([mim-assistant-context.md](mim-assistant-context.md)); you can paste that link to your assistant too, but you rarely need to.

## 6. Your part

You own the **guest slate**: who comes on, in what order, the warm outreach, and the question arc (with Rich). Rich owns everything else (tools, gear, editing, publishing). Your first dated milestone is on the calendar: **first 4 guests identified and invited by June 19.** The recording sessions are Tuesday and Thursday mornings, August 25 through September 17. You co-host every one.

## 7. If anything is unclear

Text Rich. The calendar is the source of truth; if a date moves, Rich updates the calendar and re-sends it.
