# How to record a session in Riverside

**What Riverside is:** the website where you actually record the show. Everyone (both hosts and the guest) joins a link in their web browser. Riverside records each person's camera and microphone in high quality *on their own computer*, then uploads it. That local recording is why a guest's shaky internet won't ruin the audio.

**Jargon, decoded:**
- **Studio link:** the web address guests click to join your recording room. No app, no account.
- **Separate tracks / local recording:** Riverside saves each person on their own track (their own file), recorded locally on their device. This is what makes editing clean and protects against dropouts. It's on by default on the Pro plan; you just confirm it.
- **Upload marshal:** not a Riverside feature, it's a job one host does. At the end, that host watches every person's file upload to 100% before anyone leaves.

## Before the guest joins (the 5-minute setup)

1. Open the Riverside studio link in **Google Chrome** (Chrome is the most reliable; avoid Safari).
2. When asked, **allow** microphone and camera.
3. In Riverside's device menu, pick your **Shure MV7+** as the mic (NOT the built-in laptop mic) and your **Logitech MX Brio** as the camera.
4. Put your **headphones** on (stops echo).
5. Confirm **"separate tracks / local recording" is ON** (Pro plan default).
6. Start your [QuickTime audio backup](quicktime-audio-backup.md).

## Recording

1. When everyone's ready, click **Record**.
2. Say a quick **slate**: "Mechanisms in Motion, Episode 3, take 1," then pause 3 seconds in silence. (This gives the editor a clean point to line up everyone's tracks.)
3. Have the conversation. If a guest sounds bad, ask them, live, to move closer to the screen, turn off fans/AC, and put on headphones.

## After you stop (the most important part)

1. **Do not let anyone close the tab yet.** Each person's high-quality file is still uploading.
2. The **upload marshal** opens the Riverside recordings view and confirms **every track, including the guest's, reaches 100%**.
3. If a track is stuck or missing, keep the guest on the call while it finishes. If it truly won't upload, rebook them on the spot while you have them.
4. Once all tracks are at 100%, save the Riverside files plus both QuickTime backups into the shared folder `MiM-EpNN-Guest-YYYY-MM-DD/`.
