# How to set up your kit

Both hosts have the same kit. Setting it up is mostly plugging in USB cables. Here is what each piece is and how to get it working.

**The pieces, decoded:**
- **Shure MV7+:** your microphone. **USB-C** means it plugs straight into your computer with one cable, no extra box needed. ("XLR" is the other, more complicated way to connect a mic; you are not using it.)
- **Logitech MX Brio:** your camera (a "webcam"). One USB cable.
- **Elgato Key Light Neo:** a small light so your face is well lit. USB-powered, no app needed.
- **Sony MDR-7506:** closed-back headphones (they don't leak sound, which stops echo). They plug into a jack on the back of the MV7+.
- **RODE PSA1+:** the arm that holds the mic in front of your face.
- **Reflection filter / foam:** sits behind the mic to soften room echo.

## Setup steps

1. **Mount the mic.** Clamp the RODE PSA1+ arm to your desk and screw the Shure MV7+ onto it. Position it about a fist's width from your mouth, slightly below your chin.
2. **Plug in the mic.** USB-C cable from the MV7+ to your computer.
3. **Mic settings (one time):** install the free **Shure Motiv** app, and set: **Auto Level ON** (it keeps your voice at a steady volume so you never adjust anything), light **denoise**, and **reverb OFF**. Then close the app and forget it.
4. **Headphones** into the jack on the back of the MV7+.
5. **Plug in the camera.** USB cable from the Logitech MX Brio to your computer. Clip it on top of your monitor at eye level.
6. **Light.** Plug in the Elgato Key Light Neo and place it slightly above eye level, about 45 degrees off to one side (not straight on, not behind you). Never sit with a bright window behind you.
7. **Test (30 seconds):** open [Riverside](riverside-record.md) or Descript, confirm the MV7+ and MX Brio are the selected mic and camera, say a few words, and check you look lit and sound clear.

Keep both hosts' kits identical and change parts together so the two of you always match on camera.
