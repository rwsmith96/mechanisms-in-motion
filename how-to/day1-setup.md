# Day-1 software setup (one time)

This is the one-time setup of the four accounts. Do it once, early August, and you never touch most of it again. Rich owns this; it's here so it's written down.

**The four tools, decoded:**
- **Riverside** records the show. **Descript** edits it. **Transistor** hosts and publishes it. **Calendly** lets guests book a recording time.
- **RSS feed:** a single web address Transistor creates for your show. You submit it once to Apple and Spotify, and after that every new episode shows up there automatically.
- **HLS / native video:** a newer way to put *video* (not just audio) on Apple and Spotify. It's a bonus; don't count on it. YouTube is your guaranteed video home.
- **Auto-post to YouTube:** Transistor uploads the full video to your YouTube channel for you.

## The checklist

1. **Create the four accounts** on annual billing: Riverside Pro, Descript Creator, Transistor Professional, Calendly Standard.
2. **Riverside:** turn on the **"Export to Descript"** option. If you don't see the button, email Riverside support to enable it now (not on a recording day).
3. **Transistor:** connect your **YouTube channel** so video auto-posts. If it won't connect, you can always upload to YouTube by hand.
4. **Publish a 60-second trailer** and submit the show's **RSS feed** to **Apple Podcasts Connect** and **Spotify for Creators**. Do this early: their review can take several days, and you want it done well before launch.
5. **Run one private test episode** all the way through (Transistor to Apple/Spotify/YouTube) to prove the pipe works before a real episode rides on it.
6. **Build the Calendly "Recording" event:** 75 minutes, 15-minute buffers before and after, the Riverside studio link as the location, and three intake questions (their title, their LinkedIn/bio, one Amazon-mechanism story they want to tell). See [import-calendar](import-calendar.md) for how guests use it.
7. **Write the guest prep one-pager** (a view-only Google Doc) and set up a **shared cloud folder** (Google Drive or Dropbox), one folder per episode, as the home for all recordings.
8. **Put both Rich and Carla** on each account as admins, and note the logins somewhere safe, so neither of you is ever locked out.
