# How to edit an episode in Descript

**What Descript is:** the app where you turn the raw recording into a finished episode. Its trick: it writes out a transcript of everything said, and you **edit the video by editing the text**. Delete a sentence in the transcript and the matching video disappears. That's why a non-editor can do this.

**Jargon, decoded (each is one button in Descript):**
- **Multitrack:** the recording with each person on their own track. You import it so the editor can treat each voice separately.
- **Studio Sound:** one click that cleans the audio: removes background noise, hum, and room echo, and makes voices clear. Set it to about **60–80%**, not 100% (100% can sound robotic).
- **Filler words:** the "um, uh, like, you know." Descript finds and removes them in one pass. Keep a few so it still sounds human.
- **Automatic Multicam:** Descript cuts between the cameras automatically, showing whoever is talking. You just fix the obvious misses.
- **Captions:** the on-screen subtitles, generated automatically from the transcript.
- **Clips:** short vertical highlights (for Instagram/TikTok/YouTube Shorts) that Descript can auto-generate.

## The edit, in order

1. **Import** the recording from Riverside (use the "Export to Descript" button in Riverside, or import the multitrack). Confirm you see one track per person.
2. **Studio Sound** on each track, set to **60–80%**.
3. **Edit by deleting text:** remove false starts, tangents, and dead air by deleting those words in the transcript. Then run **Remove Filler Words.**
4. **Automatic Multicam** to switch cameras, then scrub through and fix any spot where it cut away from someone still talking.
5. **Add captions** (auto-synced from the transcript).
6. **Create 2–4 clips** from the best moments. Trim each so the hook is in the first 2 seconds.
7. **Export twice:** one video for YouTube and one audio for the podcast host, using the [export settings](export-settings.md). Descript has presets for both.

**How long:** expect 4–6 hours for your first few episodes, dropping to 2–3 once you've done a handful. Editing, not recording, is the part that takes real time.

**If a guest's video looks choppy or out of sync:** their camera may have recorded at an odd frame rate. Convert their file to a steady 30fps first with the free app **HandBrake**, then import. (Ask the chatbot prompt below to walk you through it.)

## Paste this into a chatbot if you get stuck

```
I'm editing a remote two-host + one-guest video interview in Descript (Creator plan),
and I'm not a video editor. Walk me step by step through: importing the multitrack so
each person is a separate track, applying Studio Sound at 60–80%, editing by deleting
transcript text, removing filler words, running Automatic Multicam and fixing miscuts,
adding captions, making 2–4 vertical clips, and exporting a 1080p/30fps YouTube video
plus a stereo MP3 podcast file at about −14 LUFS. Tell me the exact buttons, and how to
fix a guest track that's out of sync (frame-rate issue).
```
